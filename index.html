      FAQ

      Good to know

      Is there a minimum order?

      Yes. The minimum order is , made up of any mix of boxes and menu items.

      Can I order menu items without boxes?

      Of course. Burgers, brioche sandwiches, hot dogs and salads can be ordered on their own or added to any box order.

      Can you cater for dietary requirements?

      Let us know about allergies or dietary needs in the notes field and we'll do our best to accommodate them.

      How far in advance should I order?

      Orders need at least one day's notice. For 50+ boxes we recommend ordering three days ahead.

      Do I get a discount for large orders?

      Yes. Volume discounts on boxes apply automatically in the order summary, and delivery is free above a certain order value.
